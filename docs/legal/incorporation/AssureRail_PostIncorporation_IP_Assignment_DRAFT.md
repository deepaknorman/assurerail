# Deed of assignment of AssureRail intellectual property

**Working form for execution after AssureRail incorporation and before or simultaneously with its
fundraising close — not for execution without completed schedules and advice**

**Date:** [●]

## Parties

1. **Mr Deepak Norman**, [details ●] (the **Founder**);
2. **AssureRail Private Limited** or final incorporated name, CIN [●], registered office [●]
   (**AssureRail**).

## 1. Background

1.1 Before AssureRail's incorporation, the Founder conceived, commissioned and controlled the
development of the AssureRail platform and maintained the Founder IP Register.

1.2 The Founder maintained direct ownership and control under the pre-incorporation Founder
Ownership Protocol. AssureLocker had no role in AssureRail's formation or IP-transfer chain.

1.3 AssureRail wishes to acquire directly from the Founder full title to the AssureRail-specific IP.

## 2. Assigned Rail IP

**Assigned Rail IP** means the AssureRail-specific works, software, schemas, APIs, databases,
specifications, route packs, workflows, documentation, tests, fixtures, simulations, designs,
content, marks, domains, goodwill and other items identified in the signed Schedule 1 baseline,
together with the transferable rights expressly stated there. It excludes:

- customer/provider data and third-party evidence;
- open-source and other third-party materials except assignable rights in their selection or
  arrangement;
- any AssureLocker or other third-party product code, including the full `@code/shared` package;
- AssurePool, AssurePlane and other AssureCLA product-specific logic; and
- items identified as unresolved or excluded in Schedule 3.

## 3. Founder assignment

3.1 With effect from this Deed and for the consideration in Schedule 5, the Founder irrevocably
assigns to AssureRail all of his right, title and interest in Assigned Rail IP throughout the world,
for the entire duration of each right including renewals and extensions, in all existing and future
media and forms, including rights to reproduce, adapt, translate, modify, combine, distribute,
communicate, make available, commercialise, license, sub-license, assign and enforce.

3.2 The parties expressly agree that the assignment shall not lapse merely because an assigned right
is not exercised within one year or another period, to the extent section 19(4) of the Copyright Act,
1957 applies.

3.3 To the extent permitted by law, the Founder consents to acts concerning Assigned Rail IP that
might otherwise engage a moral right and agrees not to assert that right against AssureRail or its
lawful successors and licensees. No non-waivable right is purportedly extinguished.

3.4 The Founder shall provide reasonable further assurance at AssureRail's cost to perfect,
register, defend and enforce title.

## 4. No undisclosed corporate title

4.1 The Founder warrants only on the evidence stated in the completed schedules that no company,
employer, client, agency or other person owns or controls an undisclosed right in Assigned Rail IP.

4.2 Any historic material for which AssureLocker or another person may own or claim rights must be
excluded, independently replaced or separately cured before completion. AssureLocker is not made a
party to this Deed as a substitute for that diligence.

4.3 Any future appointment of AssureLocker as an operator is a separate post-incorporation
procurement decision by AssureRail and gives AssureLocker only the express service-provider licence
in the later MSA.

## 5. Delivery and control transfer

Completion requires a signed delivery certificate covering:

- source repository and signed baseline commit/archive digest;
- build/deploy artefacts and reproducibility evidence;
- documentation, issue history and test evidence;
- domains, marks and applications;
- cloud/configuration/key/control treatment;
- contributor and licence schedules;
- customer/provider contract and data novations;
- open defects, security findings and external-evidence gates; and
- possession, access and acceptance by AssureRail.

No document shall state that a VAPT, regulatory permission, customer acceptance, production gate or
external transaction evidence has passed unless the corresponding evidence has actually been
completed and accepted.

## 6. Warranties and disclosures

The Founder gives only evidence-backed title, authority, non-encumbrance and claim warranties,
qualified by Schedule 3. The definitive deed must disclose known third-party components, contributor
gaps, security findings and customer/data restrictions. It shall not state that the Founder alone
authored all software.

## 7. Consideration, valuation, tax and FEMA

Schedule 5 must state the consideration payable to the Founder, supported by required valuation,
corporate approval, tax/GST and stamp advice. If the Founder is a person resident outside India, the
parties must obtain specific FEMA/AD-bank advice on the transfer, consideration, payment route,
pricing and reporting. This draft takes no position on whether consideration should be cash,
securities or a combination.

## 8. Corporate approvals and financing completion

AssureRail shall complete all Board, member, conflict, related-party and register requirements. The
investor documents should make satisfactory completion of this Deed, its schedules
and control delivery a condition precedent or simultaneous closing deliverable, not an unsecured
promise to clean up title later.

## 9. General

The definitive deed shall include entire agreement, amendments, severability, notices, further
assurance, counterparts/e-signing where valid, governing law and dispute resolution. Appropriate
execution, witnessing and stamp treatment must be settled by counsel.

## Schedules

1. Signed AssureRail IP baseline and manifest.
2. Excluded corporate and third-party IP.
3. Exclusions, contributor gaps, encumbrances and prior licences.
4. Third-party/open-source materials and licences.
5. Valuation, consideration and payment terms for the Founder.
6. Brands, domains, accounts and recordals.
7. Delivery and acceptance certificate.
