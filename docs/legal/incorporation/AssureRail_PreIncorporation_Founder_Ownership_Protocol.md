# AssureRail pre-incorporation founder ownership protocol

**Status:** controlling interim protocol for counsel and contributor implementation; not an IP
assignment to the future company and not an agreement with AssureLocker.

**Founder/owner pending incorporation:** Deepak Norman

**Intended transferee:** the future AssureRail Private Limited or final approved corporate name

## 1. Selected structure

1. Deepak Norman retains and directly controls the AssureRail-specific IP before incorporation.
2. AssureRail work is commissioned by or assigned directly to Deepak Norman during this period.
3. AssureLocker has no role in AssureRail's formation or IP chain and is not an interim licensee,
   development principal, repository owner, co-transferor or beneficiary.
4. Immediately after AssureRail is incorporated, Deepak Norman assigns the completed AssureRail IP
   directly to AssureRail under the post-incorporation deed and delivery certificate.
5. Only after AssureRail owns and controls the platform may it independently appoint AssureLocker or
   another supplier under a separate arm's-length services agreement.

## 2. Founder-controlled assets

Until assignment, the Founder shall maintain direct legal and administrative control of:

- AssureRail source repositories and protected branches;
- build, deployment and artefact registries;
- AssureRail domains, brand assets and any trademark applications;
- cloud subscriptions and environments used exclusively for AssureRail, or an evidenced transfer
  plan where immediate separation is not technically possible;
- signing keys, credentials and vault administration;
- product specifications, documentation, tests and fixtures;
- contributor agreements and provenance evidence; and
- customer, trustee, supplier, NDA, replay and pilot records entered into personally or through
  another accurately identified contracting party.

The Founder may appoint technical custodians as agents. Administrative access or payment of an
invoice does not give the custodian ownership.

## 3. Contributor terms

Every individual or entity producing AssureRail code, documentation, design, content or other
protectable work before incorporation must sign a written agreement with the Founder that:

1. identifies the AssureRail engagement and deliverables;
2. presently assigns the contributor's transferable rights in AssureRail-specific deliverables to
   the Founder for the specified territory, duration and rights;
3. addresses further assurance and moral rights to the extent permitted by law;
4. identifies background and third-party materials before use;
5. requires open-source and dependency records;
6. imposes confidentiality, security and return/deletion obligations; and
7. permits the Founder to assign the resulting rights to the future AssureRail company.

No new AssureRail work should be commissioned through AssureLocker. Any historic contribution for
which AssureLocker may arguably own or control rights must be isolated, replaced or cured before the
Founder gives the AssureRail title warranty. It must not be concealed by a general warranty.

## 4. Standalone repository boundary

The new AssureRail repository should contain only:

- AssureRail-owned product code and documentation;
- third-party/open-source components under their own licences; and
- narrow provider-interface contracts that AssureRail owns or is independently licensed to use.

It should not copy the AssureLocker monorepo, the full `@code/shared` package, AssurePool product
logic, AssurePlane product logic or AssureLocker customer/internal material. AssurePool and other
products should connect later through versioned, provider-neutral APIs or a narrowly licensed public
contract—not shared corporate ownership.

## 5. Customer and operational position before incorporation

1. Every agreement must name the actual legal contracting person; “AssureRail” may be described as a
   proposed product/company only where accurate.
2. No contract may imply that the unincorporated AssureRail owns assets, employs personnel, assumes
   liability or provides regulated services.
3. Pre-incorporation agreements should contain an express right and process for assignment or
   novation to AssureRail, subject to required counterparty consent.
4. Customer, trustee, depository/RTA and provider data remain governed by their agreements and are
   not owned by the Founder merely because Rail processes them.
5. Demo, replay, shadow and production labels and external-evidence gates remain accurate and
   fail-closed.

## 6. Cost and accounting register

The Founder shall separately record AssureRail pre-incorporation expenditure, payer, invoice,
purpose, payment evidence and intended treatment. AssureLocker expenses and AssureRail expenses must
not be pooled. Reimbursement, capital contribution, founder loan or assignment consideration will be
determined only after AssureRail exists and advisers confirm the treatment.

## 7. Incorporation cutover

The assignment closing should occur immediately after incorporation and before or simultaneously
with the funding close. Completion requires:

1. signed and digested repository/IP baseline;
2. contributor and third-party-rights schedule;
3. Founder-to-AssureRail assignment deed;
4. AssureRail Board acceptance and corporate records;
5. valuation, consideration, tax, FEMA and stamp treatment;
6. transfer of repository, domain, cloud, key and administrative control;
7. permitted novation/assignment of contracts and data arrangements; and
8. signed delivery and acceptance certificate.

## 8. Withdrawal of earlier approach

The earlier draft entitled `AssureRail_Interim_Founder_Licence_To_AssureLocker_DRAFT` is withdrawn
and must not be executed or relied upon. The Founder has decided that AssureLocker will have no role
in AssureRail's founding or pre-incorporation IP chain.
