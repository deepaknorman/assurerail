# AssureRail–AssureLocker operational MSA — controlling drafting instructions

**Status:** possible post-incorporation procurement model; counsel-gated legal implementation. This
MSA is not part of AssureRail's founding or Founder-to-AssureRail IP transfer.

This instruction supersedes the ownership assumption in the historical
`docs/legal/AssureLocker_AssureRail_Licence_and_IT_Outsourcing_HoT.md`. That document currently says
AssureLocker owns the AssureRail platform and licenses it to AssureRail. The selected position is the
opposite: **AssureRail owns AssureRail-specific IP.** Only after incorporation and direct delivery
from the Founder may AssureRail independently appoint AssureLocker as outsourced operator and
development/service provider.

## 1. Parties and characterisation

- Customer/platform owner: AssureRail.
- Outsourced technology and operations provider: AssureLocker.
- Related-party nature and common-control interests disclosed.
- Arm's-length scope, charges, governance and transfer-pricing evidence.
- Each regulated/material function assigned to its actual authorised performer; the MSA does not
  confer a licence or shift statutory accountability by contract.

## 2. IP model

1. AssureRail owns all Assigned Rail IP and Rail-specific foreground developments.
2. The AssureRail incorporation baseline contains no AssureLocker-owned code or full AssureLocker
   shared package.
3. AssureRail gives AssureLocker a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable service-provider licence
   for the MSA term.
4. AssureLocker services and evidence products integrate through provider-neutral APIs and narrow
   interface contracts; they are not embedded as undisclosed background code.
5. Every statement of work classifies deliverables before work begins as Rail Foreground,
   third-party material or a separately licensed provider interface.
6. No residual-knowledge clause permits AssureLocker to reproduce confidential Rail product logic.
7. No lien or suspension right may trap source, customer data, records, keys or regulatory evidence.

## 3. Service scope and reserved functions

The schedules may cover software development, maintenance, Azure hosting/operation, service desk,
integration support, security operations, release management, backup/recovery and customer technical
support. They must distinguish:

- tasks performed by AssureLocker personnel;
- decisions reserved to AssureRail management;
- participant/trustee/RTA/depository/payment-provider acts;
- prohibited functions; and
- functions requiring a licence, counsel approval or external evidence before activation.

AssureLocker may not admit participants, approve transactions, close reconciliation breaks, exercise
trustee authority, move money/securities or activate controlled-live/production modes except under a
precise authorised workflow and applicable function assignment.

## 4. Personnel, RBAC and separation

- Named role catalogue, least privilege and separate AssureRail production identities.
- AssureRail approval for privileged access; time-bound elevation and immutable access receipts.
- Maker/checker and escalation paths; no self-approval for deployments, entitlements, breaks or keys.
- Personnel screening, confidentiality, training, joiner/mover/leaver and access recertification.
- Segregation between AssureLocker assurance products and AssureRail transaction operations.
- No cross-customer or cross-product data access merely because the operator serves both products.

## 5. Data and security

- Role allocation under applicable privacy/data law decided per processing activity, not assumed
  globally.
- AssureRail customer/participant/case data used only on documented instructions.
- India-region architecture and approved subprocessor schedule, once Hyderabad/Pune primary/recovery
  roles are finally confirmed.
- Encryption, Key Vault, managed identity, WAF, network isolation, logging, vulnerability management,
  secure SDLC and incident obligations.
- Independent VAPT and remediation rights, evidence access and regulator/customer audit cooperation.
- Specific security-notification timelines aligned to AssureRail's own obligations.
- No production secrets shared with unrelated AssureLocker applications.

## 6. Service management

- Availability, performance, support coverage and severity definitions.
- Incident, problem, change, release, rollback and emergency-change procedures.
- RTO/RPO, tested restore and region-failover requirements.
- Capacity thresholds and scale testing.
- Provider/connector outage and ambiguous-external-action procedures.
- Monthly service report, control evidence and open-risk register.

## 7. Audit, continuity and exit

- AssureRail, its auditors, customers where contractually required and applicable authorities receive
  appropriate information, inspection and audit rights.
- AssureLocker flows applicable requirements to approved subprocessors and remains accountable for
  its service chain.
- Current exit plan, open formats, configuration/data/record exports, knowledge transfer and minimum
  transition assistance.
- Step-in and continuity rights, including source/build/deploy access, sufficient to answer “could
  AssureRail continue if AssureLocker failed?” with evidence.
- Deletion certification only after lawful retention and successful transfer.

## 8. Commercial terms

- Service fees are distinct from AssureRail's customer pricing. AssureRail may charge variable
  transaction fees, including the founder's currently selected PTC pricing; the MSA does not force
  Rail into a fixed-fee customer model.
- Related-party service fees require cost base, margin, allocation keys, benchmarking and periodic
  review.
- Change orders and pass-through cloud/provider costs are transparent.
- Service credits do not replace performance or remediation obligations.

## 9. Liability, insurance and termination

- Fit-for-purpose warranties tied to agreed specifications and acceptance.
- IP infringement, confidentiality, data/security, wilful misconduct and regulatory-attributable
  carve-outs considered separately from ordinary caps.
- Cyber and professional-liability insurance requirements.
- Termination for breach, security/continuity failure, insolvency, regulatory direction and
  convenience with orderly transition.
- Rights and records needed for investigations, claims, audit and retention survive.

## 10. Required schedules

1. Services and RACI.
2. Service levels, support and credits.
3. Charges and transfer-pricing method.
4. AssureRail IP, service-provider licence and provider-interface matrix.
5. Security-control schedule.
6. Data-processing and record-ownership schedule.
7. Approved locations and subprocessors.
8. RBAC, privileged access and personnel separation.
9. BCP/DR, backup and restore.
10. Incident, change and release procedures.
11. Audit, regulatory cooperation and evidence delivery.
12. Exit, transition, step-in and escrow/repository continuity.
13. Function-performer and activation-gate matrix.

## 11. Conditions before execution

- AssureRail exists, has received the completed direct Founder IP assignment and independently
  controls its repository and operational assets.
- Each company has independent authority to approve the related-party arrangement.
- Counsel confirms the regulatory/outsourcing classification against actual functions and customers.
- Tax advisers confirm pricing and related-party/transfer-pricing treatment.
- Data roles, regions and subprocessors are accurate.
- The service catalogue and RACI reflect what operations will actually do.
- Investor consent or financing-document requirements are incorporated where applicable.
