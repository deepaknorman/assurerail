# Memorandum of Association of AssureRail Private Limited

**Institutional working draft for Indian company-secretarial and legal review — not for filing or
execution as-is**

**Prepared:** 6 September 2026

**Company form assumed:** private company limited by shares

**Founder:** Deepak Norman

**Status:** placeholders, regulatory perimeter and subscriber mechanics require counsel/CS sign-off

## Drafting basis and non-negotiable intent

This working form is designed to give AssureRail durable capacity to build and commercialise
provider-neutral transaction infrastructure for direct assignments (**DA**) and pass-through
certificates (**PTC**), conventional and authorised-tokenised representations, across primary and
secondary workflows. It does not state that AssureRail is already an exchange, depository,
clearing corporation, trustee, custodian, bank, non-banking financial company, payment-system
operator, rating agency, investment adviser or other regulated person.

The filing professional must map the final text into the applicable INC-33/e-MOA fields and the
latest permitted object-code classification. The final registered-office State, approved name,
capital and subscribers remain open.

## III. Objects clause

### A. Objects to be pursued by the Company on its incorporation

1. **Transaction-infrastructure software and services.** To conceive, design, develop, own,
   acquire, configure, implement, host, operate, maintain, license, supply and support software,
   software-as-a-service, platform-as-a-service, application programming interfaces, workflow
   systems, data rooms, electronic evidence systems and other technology infrastructure for the
   origination, evaluation, diligence, structuring, documentation, transfer, assignment,
   securitisation, issue, placement, allotment, settlement coordination, record reconciliation,
   servicing, monitoring, distribution, reporting, redemption and secondary transfer or trading of
   loans, receivables, debt securities, beneficial interests and other financial or commercial
   assets, including direct-assignment and pass-through-certificate transactions, in conventional
   form or through legally permitted digital or tokenised representations.

2. **Institutional participation and governed transaction workflows.** To provide participant and
   institutional onboarding, identity and authority binding, membership, mandate, appointment,
   entitlement, maker-checker, approval, condition-precedent, communication, document-execution,
   lifecycle, exception, complaint, audit-trail, case-management and operational-control technology
   to banks, financial institutions, non-banking financial companies, trustees, asset managers,
   alternative investment funds, insurers, pension or provident funds, corporates, micro, small and
   medium enterprises, investors, arrangers, servicers, registrars and transfer agents,
   depositories, payment and settlement providers, rating agencies, assurance providers,
   professional advisers, governmental bodies, statutory authorities and other eligible persons.

3. **Discovery, terms and transaction orchestration.** Subject always to applicable law and the
   receipt of every registration, recognition, approval or licence required for the particular
   function, to provide bilateral and permissioned facilities for displaying opportunities and
   terms, invitation, discovery, request-for-quote, negotiation, allocation, matching and
   transaction orchestration, and to integrate with or appoint authorised or licensed persons to
   perform regulated distribution, arrangement, dealing, execution, clearing, settlement, custody,
   depository, registrar, trustee, payment or other functions which the Company is not itself
   authorised to perform.

4. **Evidence, data and interoperability.** To receive, validate, canonicalise, fingerprint,
   time-stamp, encrypt, retain, reconcile, transmit and controllably disclose transaction,
   ownership, payment, register, servicing, compliance, assurance and other business evidence; to
   provide evidence grading, data-quality, provenance, comparison, exception, audit-support,
   analytics, monitoring, alerting and reporting services; and to establish provider-neutral data
   contracts, technical standards, participation rules, certified connectors and interoperability
   arrangements among institutions, registries, repositories, information utilities, depositories,
   payment systems and other source or authority systems, in compliance with applicable data,
   secrecy, cybersecurity and record-retention laws.

5. **Distributed-ledger and digital-representation technology.** To research, design, develop,
   license, operate and integrate distributed-ledger, cryptographic, token, smart-contract, digital
   identity, digital-signature, key-management and verifiable-record technology, and to create or
   maintain technology representations of assets, rights, interests, instructions, events and
   evidence; provided that no such representation shall be held out as the legally authoritative
   ownership or transaction record unless the applicable legal route and competent authority permit
   that status and the Company has obtained all required approvals.

6. **Software, intellectual property and technology commercialisation.** To carry on the business
   of designing, programming, customising, testing, deploying, maintaining, licensing, marketing,
   selling, distributing, importing, exporting and otherwise dealing in computer software, mobile
   and web applications, artificial-intelligence and machine-learning systems, models and agents,
   databases, cybersecurity tools and related technology and managed services; and to acquire,
   create, register, protect, hold, license, sub-license, assign and otherwise exploit copyrights,
   patents, trademarks, designs, domain names, algorithms, models, know-how, confidential
   information, trade secrets and other intellectual property connected with the Company's business.

7. **Advisory, implementation and support ancillary to technology.** To provide implementation,
   integration, testing, migration, training, certification, operational-support, business-process,
   research, technical-consulting and programme-management services incidental to the foregoing
   technology objects, but not to provide a regulated credit rating, audit opinion, investment
   advice, legal opinion, trusteeship or other reserved professional service except through an
   appropriately qualified and authorised person and in accordance with applicable law.

8. **Regulatory perimeter.** Nothing in these objects authorises the Company, without first
   obtaining and maintaining the required registration, recognition, approval or licence, to carry
   on banking or the business of a non-banking financial company; accept public deposits; operate a
   payment system; hold client money or securities; provide custody; act as a debenture trustee,
   securitisation trustee, registrar and transfer agent, depository, stock exchange, electronic
   trading platform, clearing corporation, credit rating agency, investment adviser, portfolio
   manager, broker, merchant banker, collective investment scheme, insurance intermediary or other
   regulated intermediary; make a public offer; or undertake any activity reserved to a person
   authorised by the Reserve Bank of India, Securities and Exchange Board of India, International
   Financial Services Centres Authority, Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India,
   Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority, Ministry of Corporate Affairs or any other
   competent authority. The Company may support such functions as a technology provider or
   outsourced service provider and may contract with authorised performers, only within the
   applicable legal and contractual perimeter.

## Matters counsel and the company secretary must settle before filing

1. Name availability, registered-office State and the correct jurisdictional filing treatment.
2. Whether a standard private company or an OPC is intended. Section 3 of the Companies Act, 2013
   requires two or more persons to form a private company; economic ownership, beneficial ownership
   and subscriber disclosures must be true and must not be obscured by an informal nominee device.
3. Authorised/issued capital and equity/preference split aligned to the fundraising instrument,
   ESOP pool, valuation, stamp and filing costs.
4. Whether any object wording triggers a regulator or Registrar clarification and whether a more
   conservative filing version should be paired with this long-form governance record.
5. Whether transaction-linked fees, discovery, matching, secondary workflows or tokenised
   representation require a licensed partner or approval before activation. The objects clause is
   capacity, not permission.
6. Final NIC/activity codes and the exact text accepted by the MCA filing utility.
