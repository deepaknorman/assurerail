## 1. Purpose and precedence

Schedules, accepted change orders, data-processing terms and security schedule form the entire
pilot contract. If they conflict, the negotiated order of precedence is: **[DPA/security schedule]
→ [Agreement] → [Statement of Work] → [other]**.

## 2. Defined engagement modes

- **Demonstration:** a private walkthrough using labelled synthetic data only.
- **Replay:** observation and reconstruction of a completed transaction; the original transaction
  and its records remain unchanged and authoritative.
- **Shadow:** observation beside a current incumbent process; the incumbent and named participants
  remain authoritative and perform every operative act.
- **Controlled-live:** excluded unless a later signed change order identifies each function,
  performer, permission, external dependency and activation evidence.

Only the selected mode in Schedule 1 is authorised. No mode upgrades automatically.

## 3. Services and exclusions

AssureRail will provide the expressly listed software access, workshops, evidence mapping,
workflow configuration, reporting and support. Unless a signed controlled-live change order says
otherwise, AssureRail does not:

- solicit investors or borrowers, recommend a transaction, provide investment/credit/legal/tax
  advice, or make the Customer's credit or investment decision;
- act as trustee, arranger, rating agency, RTA, depository, custodian, escrow agent, account bank,
  payment system, clearing corporation or assurance provider;
- receive, hold, control or transfer money, securities, title, custody assets or private signing
  keys;
- issue, allot, match, execute, settle or register a transaction; or
- represent that a Rail record or token is the legally operative ownership record.

## 4. Customer authority and dependencies

Customer warrants that it has authority to enter the pilot and provide each item of data or
evidence. It will name business, transaction-file, data/privacy, information-security and decision
owners; obtain permissions from other participants; identify the applicable authoritative records;
and ensure its users act within valid mandates. AssureRail may pause work where authority, evidence,
security or external dependencies are absent or contradictory.

## 5. Data scope and handling

Before any non-synthetic data is received, the parties will sign Schedule 2 identifying purpose,
data controller/processor roles, permitted fields, prohibited fields, source owner, transfer
channel, access list, location, retention, legal hold, return/destruction and incident contacts.
Email and the public enquiry form are not approved transaction-data channels. Missing evidence is
recorded as missing; AssureRail need not manufacture, infer or substitute it.

The parties will execute a data-processing addendum where required. Subprocessors, cross-border
transfers and material location changes require the notices/approvals stated in that addendum.

## 6. Security and access

Each party will apply reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to
its role. AssureRail will use named access, least privilege, environment separation, encryption,
logging, vulnerability management, backup/recovery and incident procedures as specified in the
security schedule. Customer will protect credentials, promptly revoke departed users and notify
suspected compromise.

No statement in a proposal or demonstration constitutes a security certification. Independent VAPT
or customer testing occurs only under a written testing authorisation defining target, time,
methods, contacts, data and disclosure.

## 7. Evidence and outputs

Outputs may distinguish expected, received, verified and reconciled information and will retain
material qualifications. They are limited to the agreed sources, dates and purpose. A signature or
hash may establish origin/integrity but does not establish completeness, legal effect or correctness
of the underlying source. Customer remains responsible for its decisions and reliance.

## 8. Fees, tax and payment

Fees, fee base, trigger, minimum/cap, expenses, third-party charges, taxes, invoice timing, payment
period, disputed amounts, cancellation and refund/credit treatment are in Schedule 3. Fees for an
independent assurance provider are separately appointed and disclosed. No rate is implied by a
demonstration or public statement.

## 9. Intellectual property

Each party retains its background IP. Subject to payment, Customer receives a non-exclusive,
non-transferable, revocable right to use the pilot service for the agreed internal purpose and term.
AssureRail owns its software, generic schemas, methods and improvements, excluding Customer data and
Customer confidential information. No licence to train a general model on Customer data is granted.
Feedback treatment is **[to be negotiated]** and may not expose Customer confidential information.

## 10. Confidentiality and publicity

Each party will protect the other's confidential information, use it only for the pilot and disclose
it only to personnel/advisers with a need to know and equivalent duties. Standard exclusions and
legally compelled disclosure procedures apply. Neither party may use the other's name, logo,
transaction, results, quotation or relationship publicly without prior written approval for the
specific use. Default meeting recording and screenshots: prohibited.

## 11. Service levels, changes and support

Pilot availability and support objectives are in Schedule 1 and are not production SLAs. Changes to
route, data scope, integrations, users, operating mode or external actions require written change
control. Emergency safe-pause may take precedence over availability. Planned maintenance,
third-party failure and Customer-caused events are handled as negotiated.

## 12. Compliance, conflicts and responsible use

Each party will comply with applicable law, anti-bribery, sanctions and acceptable-use duties. The
function/performer matrix will disclose participant-owned, external-authority and separately
appointed functions. Related-party technology or operating services, if any, are disclosed in the
applicable schedule. Neither party will use the pilot to mislead a regulator, investor, borrower,
auditor or transaction participant.

The current organisational plan contemplates AssureLocker Pvt Ltd. providing specified operational
and Plaza services to AssureRail under a written MSA. That arrangement is not effective merely
because this template mentions it. If it applies to the pilot, Schedule 1 and the data/security
schedules must identify the precise services, data access, subprocessor role, service levels,
security obligations, incident responsibility, audit/exit rights and related-party pricing. It
confers no transaction authority on AssureLocker or Plaza and no ownership of AssureRail customer
relationships, transaction data or IP.

## 13. Warranties and disclaimers

Each party warrants authority to contract. AssureRail warrants it will perform the agreed pilot with
reasonable skill and care. Except as expressly agreed, pilot and diagnostic outputs are provided
without a warranty of transaction eligibility, legal effectiveness, credit performance, investment
return, uninterrupted operation or fitness for a production transaction.

## 14. Liability, indemnities and insurance

Counsel must negotiate: excluded losses; aggregate cap; enhanced caps or uncapped liabilities for
confidentiality, data protection, fraud, wilful misconduct, IP infringement and amounts that cannot
lawfully be limited; third-party claims; mitigation; and available cyber/professional insurance.
No placeholder cap is accepted by silence.

## 15. Term, suspension, termination and exit

The pilot starts and ends on the dates in Schedule 1. Either party may terminate for uncured material
breach, insolvency, illegality or agreed convenience notice. AssureRail may immediately suspend an
unsafe, unauthorised or legally prohibited activity. On exit, access is revoked and Customer data is
returned/exported or securely deleted according to Schedule 2, subject to legal hold and the agreed
minimal audit record.

## 16. Incidents, complaints, audit and regulatory requests

Schedules identify incident notice targets, escalation contacts, complaint handling, evidence
preservation and audit rights. Each party handles regulator/law-enforcement requests applicable to
its role and coordinates where lawful. Access to systems, code, security reports or other customers'
information is never implied by a generic audit clause.
