# AssureRail PUB-00 public-claims evidence

**Status:** implementation evidence, 3 September 2026

**Scope:** immediate correction of materially false or unsafe public claims; not the later
evidence-led publication expansion.

## Corrections made

- AssureRail is described as provider-neutral DA/PTC transaction infrastructure across conventional
  and authorised-tokenised representations.
- Built/deployed software is kept distinct from enabled, customer-accepted, controlled-live and
  production capability.
- The eventual AssureRail company, IP, funding and licence structure is stated as counsel-gated; an
  incorporated subsidiary is not presented as an existing fact.
- AssurePool is stated as DA-only tape preparation. PTC orchestration and tokenised representation
  do not belong to AssurePool.
- External banks, trustees, payment providers and route-defined recordkeepers retain their regulated
  acts and legal authority.
- The old token-first AssureRail and pool-tokenisation web briefings are withdrawn. The dated RBI
  deck is visibly marked as historic decision material.

## Automated evidence


The check asserts the mandatory boundaries on the current AssureRail landing page, the AssureLocker
downloads and investor surfaces, and the legacy-briefing withdrawal notices. It also rejects the
most material old current-state formulations from active surfaces.

## Deliberate limit

PUB-00 does not claim to rewrite every historic deck, article or speaker note. Historic material can
remain only when it is withdrawn or prominently labelled as superseded. PUB-01 and PUB-02 publish
new route/persona/trust material only when the corresponding replay and acceptance evidence exists.
