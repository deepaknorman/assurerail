AssureRail
The problemOne transaction, many partial truths

The deal is not trapped in one bad system. It is fragmented across many good ones.

LMS, arranger, trustee, rating, document, payment, depository and participant books each do a necessary job. None gives every accountable party the same governed transaction state.

Repeated diligence

The same pool, documents and exceptions are rebuilt for each party and each transaction.

Completion ambiguity

“Done” can mean signed, funded, allotted, registered or merely updated on one internal screen.

Reconciliation debt

Tape, contract, payment, trustee and holding records drift until operations resolves them manually.

Lifecycle fragmentation

Servicing, waterfalls, triggers, reporting and secondary changes split into email and spreadsheets.

The opportunity is not to replace every system. It is to create one controlled transaction case across them.
AssureRail begins as a coexistence and reconciliation layer. Incumbent systems remain authoritative during replay and shadow.

Trust modelAssurance organised by the trustee

AssureRail does not mark its own transaction.

1 · Appoint

The trustee or other accountable party selects the independent assurer/reviewer. It may choose AssurePlane in the AssureCLA suite, another provider or its own permitted process.

2 · Validate

AssureRail checks that the required conclusion is signed, scoped, current, attributable and in the route-defined schema.

3 · Preserve

AssureRail records the provider, appointment, scope, qualifications, exceptions and supersession without upgrading the claim.

No commercial employee can alter an assurance result. Related-party use is disclosed. Provider substitution and export are tested.
Provider-neutral assurance is a binding product rule, not a promise that depends on AssurePlane or AssureLocker availability.

Pilot proofFour parts · separately reviewable

Prove the transaction before asking anyone to trust the venue.

1 · Completed-deal replay

One closed DA and one closed PTC. Reconstruct roles, evidence, decisions, state, breaks and completion.

Zero effect on a live transaction.

2 · Parallel shadow

Run one live conventional DA and PTC beside the incumbent process; produce comparison and exception workbooks.

Incumbent remains authoritative.

3 · Operational proof

Exercise missing data, duplicate events, signer failure, register mismatch, settlement break, restore and full export.

Proves accuracy, resilience and exit.

4 · Controlled conversion

One net-new conventional transaction under counsel-approved roles, contracts and licensed-partner hand-offs.

Only after Parts 1–3 and readiness gates.

The programme can stop after any part. Progression is earned by signed evidence, not by a calendar promise.

Both DA and PTC lead at replay/shadow. The first controlled-live route is selected only after the evidence and operating model are reviewed.
