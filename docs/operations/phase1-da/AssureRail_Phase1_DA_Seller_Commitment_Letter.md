## 3. Free Tier-A tape pre-screen authority

The Seller authorises the named AssureRail project lead and, if appointed, the named Data Preparer to use the Seller-provided loan tape solely to perform one free Tier-A eligibility pre-screen for this Seller and analytical cohort. The pre-screen may apply recorded rules to origination date and original tenor for minimum-holding analysis (**CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL**), residual tenor, loan or asset type against a stated buyer class, days-past-due history, restructuring flags, borrower concentration and cross-seller borrower overlap.

This limited authority becomes effective only when the Seller signs Signature Gate A below. It may be signed before the Tier-A ranges and cohort allocation fields are completed. Those fields must then be completed from the resulting worksheet before Signature Gate B is presented.

This authority is subject to all of the following limits:

1. The input is a tape only. No loan agreement, KYC file, RC, insurance policy, security document or other evidence file is requested or reviewed at this stage.
2. Direct personal identifiers must be removed or replaced with consistent seller-generated tokens before transfer, except where a separately approved data instrument expressly permits otherwise. A cross-seller overlap check uses controlled pseudonymous keys and does not merge ownership or borrower records.
3. The pre-screen is free. Any Data Preparer formatting charge is a separate Seller expense agreed directly with that provider and is not an AssureRail fee.
4. The pre-screen may report a range and classify exclusions as time-cured, remediable or structural. It cannot establish legal transferability, compliance, enforceability, buyer acceptance or final eligibility.
5. Minimum-holding treatment, required seller retention and permitted-transferee status remain **CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL**. No unconfirmed regulatory figure is incorporated into this letter as a fact.
6. The authority does not permit contact with a buyer in the Seller's name, disclosure of the Seller's tape or results to a buyer, negotiation, price acceptance, submission of a paid assessment, or any instruction affecting money, title, security or servicing.
7. The Seller may revoke this authority in writing. On revocation or expiry at `[date/event]`, recipients must stop processing and follow the applicable data-return or deletion terms, subject to lawful retention.

Tier-B matters require admitted evidence and the later paid, full-population workflow. They may include hypothecation endorsement, insurance in force and any required hypothecation clause, borrower KYC and authority, stamping and agreement execution. No Tier-B conclusion is produced under this letter.

## 7. Later binding documents; no automatic conversion

After AssureRail Private Limited is incorporated, a paid Initial Assessment may proceed only when all of the following occur:

1. the incorporated company completes its legal, tax, banking, security and operational readiness gates;
2. the incorporated company and Seller execute the Seller MSA;
3. the incorporated company issues an Initial Assessment Order stating the final Seller scope, counts, corpus, price, GST, data terms, expiry and automated-service limits;

5. the applicable upfront invoice is paid through the approved company payment route.

The parties state their present intention to complete those documents using the recorded terms, subject to lawful changes and the stated quote validity. This letter does not automatically convert, novate or become the binding IA Order upon incorporation. Silence, incorporation, tape delivery or completion of the free pre-screen is not acceptance of paid work.

Portfolio Preparation, buyer-facing execution and settlement require their own later SOW, Execution Mandate, provider appointments, buyer documents and seller-authorised settlement schedule. No such authority is granted here.
