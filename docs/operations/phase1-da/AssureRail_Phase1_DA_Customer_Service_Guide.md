# AssureRail conventional DA: customer service guide

**Version:** 1.1 — 17 September 2026
**Audience:** prospective NBFC sellers and approved buyer-design partners
**Availability:** Initial Assessment applications are open for approved NBFC portfolios. AssureRail Private Limited is in formation; commercial contracting and paid processing begin only after incorporation and the applicable readiness gates have passed. Live transfer, funds movement and settlement services remain subject to separate institutional activation.

Conventional DA is the Phase 1 customer route. PTC is Phase 2 and remains in discovery and design; it is not included in this offer.

## A prepared route from book to buyer

AssureRail gives a seller a staged way to test, improve and prepare a loan portfolio before formal buyer diligence. The seller first receives an automated view of data quality, evidence coverage, eligibility, risk and indicative economics. If it proceeds, qualified professionals review the relevant Portfolio Preparation sections before the buyer process begins.

### 1. Initial Assessment

The seller confirms the proposed portfolio, unique loan and party counts, corpus and selected scope, accepts the quote and pays the Initial Assessment amount. The platform then processes the submitted loan tape and documents, records rule outcomes across the admitted population, identifies evidence gaps and returns preliminary economics and a remediation plan.

The Initial Assessment is automated, unsigned and preliminary. It does not include a consultant, qualified expert or human content reviewer. It is designed to reveal avoidable work before the seller commits to professional diligence and execution costs.

AssureRail reads machine-readable files and usable native document text with bounded software libraries first. Configured AI reads only the document fields or pages that need it, and each extracted material field retains its evidence reference and state: observed, inferred, absent, unreadable or contradictory. Deterministic controls then compare the observed evidence with the admitted loan tape. Inferred values do not establish a pass, and AI does not make the buyer's legal, credit or purchase decision.

The pilot quote and accepted order state the included automated reassessment allowance and its time window. Corrected evidence may be uploaded and reassessed without closing the workspace.

### 2. Portfolio Preparation

If the seller proceeds, AssureRail reconciles the admitted pool, resolves material exceptions, prepares the buyer-ready evidence structure and coordinates qualified section review. A CA or eligible financial reviewer and transaction counsel review and sign their own sections. An asset or technical specialist is used only where the asset class, evidence or exception rules require one.

This stage prepares the case for buyer review. It does not replace the buyer's credit, legal, compliance, valuation or purchase decision.

### 3. Execution

The seller gives a defined execution mandate. AssureRail coordinates the selected provider and buyer workstreams, the question-and-answer record, conditions, settlement evidence and seller-authorised distribution schedule. The success fee is based on the seller's actual purchase consideration successfully settled and is due only on a successful closing.

Settlement money moves through the appointed bank or settlement provider. AssureRail does not receive gross purchase consideration into its own account or control customer money.

## Buyer journey

An institutional buyer enters through a separate controlled route. After its buyer MSA is signed, AssureRail creates the buyer workspace and invites its nominated administrators. The buyer records its requirements through structured checkboxes, ranges and controlled selections, approves scoped user roles, and completes a synthetic workspace test before receiving a case invitation.

For an invited case, the buyer receives only seller-approved evidence, raises traceable diligence questions and records its own conditions and purchase decision. Its credit, legal, compliance, valuation and pricing responsibilities stay with the buyer. Settlement and optional monitoring access are separately scoped; neither follows merely from viewing a portfolio.

## How the quote is built

AssureRail reviews the declared corpus, unique seller-loan-borrower or co-borrower pairs, linked parties, asset type, evidence state, buyer requirements and selected work before issuing the quote. Any difference found during admission is recalculated; there is no quantity or corpus tolerance band.

The quote separates:

- AssureRail stage fees;
- selected managed services;
- third-party or professional services;
- statutory charges and duties; and
- conditional reserves that activate only when the stated condition occurs.

We provide a case-specific quote rather than a public target percentage. Routine managed services may use a stated AssureRail customer rate. Statutory amounts and exceptional actual-cost items remain separately visible.

## Monitoring choice

Every executed case receives a one-time closing baseline. Ongoing monitoring is optional and begins only when selected in the quote or mandate. For a DA, the normal allocation is seller-funded during year one and buyer-funded from year two, unless the seller remains the servicer or the transaction documents state another allocation.

## What each party keeps

- The seller confirms data, evidence, authority, representations and settlement instructions.
- The buyer retains its own diligence, approval, pricing and purchase decision.
- Counsel, auditors, bureau providers, banks and registries remain responsible for their own acts.
- AssureRail coordinates the accepted workflow, records evidence and produces the stated deliverables within the contracted boundary.

No service configuration guarantees a buyer, price, timing, eligibility outcome or closing.
