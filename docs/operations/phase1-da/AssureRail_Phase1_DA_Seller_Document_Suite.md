# AssureRail Phase 1 DA seller document suite

**Version:** 1.0 — 16 September 2026
**Classification:** `AUTHENTICATED`
**Status:** drafting structure for Indian counsel review; this is not an executed agreement or legal opinion.

## Document architecture

The seller signs a durable master agreement and short, versioned orders for each paid stage. Execution authority and settlement instructions remain separate because they carry different dependencies and approval risks.

| Document | Commercial function | Required schedules | Signature trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seller Master Services Agreement (MSA) | Common legal, data, security, liability, IP, audit, dispute and order rules | Definitions; security/data schedule; acceptable use; support; provider/pass-through framework | After AssureRail incorporation and before the first paid order |
| Initial Assessment Order | Binding declared scope, automated service, quote and payment | Seller/entity; asset class; corpus; unique-pair counts; linked-party counts; required uploads; run allowance; exclusions; quote validity | Before Initial Assessment payment and data admission |
| Portfolio Preparation Statement of Work (SOW) | Preparation deliverables, professional sections, assumptions, limits and fixed-stage balance | Admitted pool; remediation baseline; reviewer assignments; buyer profile; timetable; dependencies; additional-service lines | Before Portfolio Preparation starts |
| Execution Mandate | Defined authority to coordinate selected execution work and earn the seller-specific success fee | Buyer/case; mandate period; fee basis; service selections; provider allocation; Q&A; closing conditions; conversion credit; termination | Before buyer-facing execution and provider activation |
| Settlement Distribution Schedule | Seller-authorised closing-day payment waterfall and account details | Gross consideration; seller share; debt release; statutory/provider amounts; earned AssureRail fee; taxes; residual seller proceeds; bank evidence | Locked only after executed transaction documents, final figures and maker-checker verification |

## Seller MSA minimum clauses

1. **Parties and activation.** AssureRail Private Limited must exist and be capable of contracting. No pre-incorporation document should imply that the proposed company accepted a paid engagement or live mandate.
2. **Order precedence.** The MSA controls common terms; an accepted order controls case scope and price; the execution mandate controls execution authority; the locked settlement schedule controls only the stated closing distribution.
3. **Service boundary.** AssureRail provides the accepted platform, preparation, coordination and evidence services. The seller owns its data and representations. The buyer owns its diligence and decision. External professionals and authoritative providers own their acts.
4. **No outcome promise.** No buyer, price, eligibility, timing, registry result or closing is guaranteed.
5. **Data and security.** Purpose, access, retention, subprocessors, incident handling, export and deletion. Data Preparers and referral partners receive no implied access.
6. **Confidentiality and use.** Seller-private, buyer-private, shared-case and professional-privilege classes; permitted disclosure and clean-team restrictions.
7. **AI and automated processing.** Initial Assessment is automated and unsigned. Model/rule version, evidence references, limitations, human escalation for processing integrity and prohibited reliance are stated.
8. **Professional review.** Portfolio Preparation uses qualified section reviewers. Each reviewer signs only its assigned section; AssureRail does not convert those sections into a whole-case opinion.
9. **Fees, taxes and providers.** Orders distinguish AssureRail fees, managed services, third-party/professional costs, statutory amounts and conditional reserves.
10. **Customer money.** Gross purchase consideration is paid to the appointed bank/provider. AssureRail has no custody or unilateral payment authority.
11. **Audit and evidence.** Versioned orders, access receipts, run records, reviewer deliverables, provider receipts and settlement reconciliation.
12. **Liability, remedies and insurance.** Separate platform/coordination failures from professional, bank, registry, seller-data and buyer-decision failures.
13. **Suspension and termination.** Evidence/security breach, illegality, non-payment, mandate withdrawal, provider failure and orderly data/export treatment.
14. **Complaints and disputes.** Operational escalation, good-faith resolution, governing law and venue selected by counsel.

## Initial Assessment Order

The order records the seller, legal authority, portfolio cut-off, asset type, declared corpus, unique seller-loan-borrower/co-borrower pairs, separate loan-linked-party pairs and selected scope. It states the complete standalone fixed-stage quote, the Initial Assessment amount due, quote expiry and exact reconciliation rule.

Required customer wording:

> The Initial Assessment is an automated, unsigned and preliminary output. It does not include a consultant, qualified expert or human content reviewer and is not a legal opinion, audit, valuation, credit approval or promise of transfer.

The order states the included number of same-scope automated reassessments and their availability window. A change in book, asset class or material scope is not a reassessment.

## Portfolio Preparation SOW

The SOW identifies:

- admitted population and control totals;
- known gaps and remediation ownership;
- each prepared output and buyer-facing schedule;
- the CA/financial and transaction-counsel sections and named eligible reviewers;
- any triggered asset/technical or field review;
- whether an independent buyer-reliance certificate is separately required;
- seller, provider and buyer dependencies;
- service clock, pause rules and acceptance criteria; and
- every selected additional service, provider, payer and limit.

The seller pays the remaining fixed-stage amount before work begins. A provider change or extra professional procedure requires a written change order when it changes price, timing, reliance or output.

## Execution Mandate

The mandate is seller-specific even when multiple sellers contribute to a cohort. It states the target buyer or approved buyer class, corpus, mandate period, Q&A authority, selected providers, selected Base/Premium/Full lines, success-fee policy version and seller-specific calculation basis.

The mandate must not appoint AssureRail as lender, buyer, escrow agent, trustee, legal adviser, auditor, bureau user, statutory filer or holder of customer money unless a later expressly authorised service and legal review support that role. Voluntary switch or withdrawal and any committed-route top-up are stated precisely; buyer rejection or a failed closing alone does not trigger that top-up.

## Settlement Distribution Schedule

The schedule is a closing control, not a broad power of attorney. It contains:

| Field | Control |
|---|---|
| Purchase consideration | Buyer-approved actual amount and seller allocation |
| Existing-debt release | Current payoff letter, beneficiary validation and release condition |
| Statutory and provider payments | Final invoice/receipt, payer allocation and unused-reserve treatment |
| AssureRail fee | Earned stage/success amount, tax invoice, approved conversion credit and seller allocation |
| Residual proceeds | Seller bank account independently verified |
| Maker-checker | Seller authorised maker and checker; AssureRail finance maker/checker; bank acknowledgement |
| Validity | Closing date/cut-off; any change requires reapproval |
| Reconciliation | Bank/VAN receipts, unmatched or failed payment process, final closing statement |

The buyer may fund a VAN or settlement account identified by the appointed bank. The bank executes only the operative instruction accepted under its own mandate. AssureRail prepares and reconciles the schedule but cannot release or redirect funds on its own.

## Buyer-side companion documents

The seller suite depends on a separate buyer MSA and onboarding record covering buyer institution admission, approved users, scoped RBAC, requirement-profile selections, case invitations, confidentiality, permitted data use, diligence/Q&A rules and settlement acknowledgements. Buyer onboarding must occur after the formal buyer MSA, and selection fields should use controlled multiple choice, checkboxes and ranges wherever practical.
