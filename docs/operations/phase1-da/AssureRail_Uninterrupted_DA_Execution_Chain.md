# AssureRail conventional DA — uninterrupted execution chain

**Operating instruction:** execute continuously by dependency and evidence gate. Calendar weeks
and target dates do not control sequencing. External activations remain subject to the named
party's authority and acceptance.

## Outcome

Take one seller from a paid, automated Initial Assessment through correction and reassessment,
expert-reviewed Portfolio Preparation, buyer review and a rehearsed coordinated closing. Produce
commit-tied technical evidence and measured commercial outcomes at each irreversible gate.

## Commercial interpretation of the 750-record validation slice

The validation ladder controls how much representative data the model is allowed to claim against;
it does not create a separate discount. For a seller below the fixed-stage minima and without a
large-programme supplement:

- committed route total for Initial Assessment plus Portfolio Preparation: **₹8.00L**;
- standalone route total: **₹10.40L**;
- Initial Assessment paid before processing: **₹3.12L**, being 30% of the standalone quote;
- committed Portfolio Preparation balance: **₹4.88L**;
- standalone Portfolio Preparation balance: **₹7.28L**.

All figures exclude applicable tax, selected additional services, third-party costs and execution
success fees. The admitted count and corpus are reconciled without a tolerance band.

For either of the first two independently approved design-partner NBFC entities, the standard quote
remains visible and a lifetime 30% invoice credit applies to the eligible core fees. At the same
minimum slice this gives ₹5.60L committed, ₹7.28L standalone and a ₹2.184L Initial Assessment
invoice before GST. The discount is entity-bound and does not apply to the current pilot exclusion
set of ancillary services, pass-throughs, SFTP, API work, monitoring or contractor day rates.

## External inputs that do not stop internal work

- Sevenfincorp supplies a named seller, authority and representative material.
- HDFC and Axis supply their own criteria and integration requirements; synthetic profiles are
  used until then.
- The founder contracts qualified Portfolio Preparation reviewers.
- Razorpay, the selected bank/escrow provider and each buyer activate credentials or sandboxes.
- Live invoicing, money movement, regulator contact and customer-data use remain separately
  authorised actions.

## Remaining integration truth

- Fixed-stage design-partner credit is integrated with invoice preparation, Razorpay test checkout
  and paid-stage gating. The execution success-fee invoice is persisted, seller-specific and bound
  to an immutable closing reference and independently reviewed service-fee leg. Provider-side
  composition and settlement observation remain external integration gates.
- Real buyer criteria, SFTP endpoints and acknowledgement formats must come from HDFC, Axis or the
  selected buyer. The adapter stays in sandbox until that acceptance exists.
- Live escrow/VAN instructions require the selected provider's API and operating acceptance. The
  current closing pack is deterministic rehearsal evidence and does not move funds.

## Release handoff

Codex produces cohesive local commits, test evidence and a limitations register. Claude reviews
security, strategy/architecture consistency and outward claims, then performs the authorised
release. No manual test count is treated as maturity evidence without the associated commit and
machine-readable result.
