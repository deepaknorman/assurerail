AssureRail Phase 1 DA referral partner addendum

Version: 1.0 — 16 September 2026 Classification: AUTHENTICATED Status: commercial drafting instructions for counsel; becomes effective only when executed by AssureRail Private Limited and the referral partner.

Pilot counterparty schedule: Sevenfincorp, subject to confirmation of its exact legal name, registered details, GST status and authorised signatory before execution. The operative agreement must use the verified legal entity rather than the trading name alone.

1. Purpose and appointment

The referral partner may identify and introduce prospective institutional sellers for AssureRail's conventional DA services. It is an independent contractor. It may not bind AssureRail, promise a buyer or closing, quote an unapproved price, collect AssureRail fees, give regulated/professional advice on AssureRail's behalf or access a seller or buyer workspace merely because it made an introduction.

2. Accepted registration

A prospect is registered only after AssureRail accepts a written registration that identifies the legal institution, relevant contact, expected portfolio and date. Acceptance is prospective, non-exclusive and subject to prior relationship, duplicate, conflict, consent and eligibility checks.

An accepted registration lasts six months. It lapses automatically if the partner has not produced an evidenced qualifying introduction within 60 days of acceptance. A later introduction requires a new registration and written acceptance; the old registration does not revive automatically.

An evidenced qualifying introduction means a consented, attributable connection to an authorised or relevant decision maker, recorded in AssureRail's approved system with enough information for qualification. A name, list purchase, forwarded public contact, unaccepted event attendance or unsupported claim does not qualify.

3. Referral fee

The base referral fee is 8.5%, unless the executed addendum or an accepted prospect-specific registration states another mutually agreed rate.

The fee applies only to collected AssureRail fees for:

  1. Initial Assessment;
  2. Portfolio Preparation; and
  3. core Execution success service.

It does not apply to arrangement or ancillary services, integrations, large-programme supplements, monitoring, supplier or professional fees, statutory charges, duties, GST or other taxes, third-party pass-throughs, refunds, credits, write-offs or amounts not collected by AssureRail. The stated referral percentage is GST-inclusive for the partner's referral service; GST is not added again to that percentage. The executed tax clause remains subject to counsel and tax review.

The partner earns no fee merely because a quote, order or invoice exists. AssureRail calculates the fee after eligible customer money is received and retained, net of applicable credit, refund, reversal or bad debt. A later credit or refund may be netted from the next referral statement.

4. Statement, invoice and payment

Referral fees are accounted for quarterly in arrears. AssureRail provides a statement of eligible receipts, exclusions and adjustments. The partner issues a valid invoice against the accepted statement. Payment is subject to applicable TDS, GST and tax-document requirements and the payment period in the executed addendum.

No advance, minimum, exclusivity payment or fee on non-eligible revenue is implied.

5. Data preparation is separate

If the seller appoints the partner as a Data Preparer, that work is a separate seller-paid arrangement. It may cover ingest formatting and upload of assigned seller data. The Data Preparer cannot attest to seller information, accept a quote, pay, submit an assessment, approve a report, enter an execution mandate, view buyer-private information or make a transaction decision.

Seller workspace access requires the seller's explicit invitation, least-privilege role, confidentiality terms and expiry. Referral status alone grants no access.

6. Conduct and claims

The partner must use current approved material and clearly state AssureRail's activation status. It must not claim that AssureRail guarantees eligibility, buyer interest, regulatory treatment, price, time or closing. It must not describe automated Initial Assessment as expert-reviewed or describe Portfolio Preparation as a buyer approval.

Conflicts, gifts, inducements, complaints, information-security incidents and suspected misconduct are disclosed promptly. The partner keeps evidence of consented introductions and does not upload borrower personal data for referral qualification.

7. Termination and surviving rights

Either party may terminate under the executed addendum. Termination stops new registrations and access. An already accepted registration may continue only for its remaining window and only if the addendum expressly preserves it. Accrued referral fees on eligible amounts collected before the applicable end date remain subject to statement, invoice, tax and adjustment rules.

Confidentiality, data protection, audit, tax, repayment of overpayment, dispute and conduct obligations survive as drafted by counsel.
