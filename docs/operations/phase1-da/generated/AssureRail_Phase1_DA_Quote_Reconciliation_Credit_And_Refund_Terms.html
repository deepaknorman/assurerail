AssureRail Phase 1 DA quote, reconciliation, credit and refund terms

Version: 1.0 — 16 September 2026 Classification: SHARED_PASSWORD Status: customer commercial schedule for counsel and tax review before use.

1. Quote basis

AssureRail reviews the proposed asset class, seller, corpus, counts, evidence state, intended buyer route and selected services before issuing a binding quote. The quote is case-specific and identifies its commercial-policy version.

The primary fixed-stage quantity is each unique seller × loan × borrower or co-borrower combination. Each separate linked party for a loan is a linked-party quantity. Duplicate rows do not create a second unit; distinct co-borrowers and linked parties do.

The quote states the complete standalone fixed-stage amount, any committed-route amount, Initial Assessment payment, selected additional services, third-party/professional estimates, statutory items, tax and conditions.

2. Quote validity

Unless the quote states a shorter period, it expires at 5:00 PM IST on the first Monday after the twentieth calendar day following issue. No system assumption extends that time silently.

Acceptance locks the unit rates and policy version for the accepted order. It does not freeze an inaccurate quantity, corpus or changed service scope.

3. Initial payment and exact reconciliation

The Initial Assessment amount is 30% of the complete standalone fixed-stage quote. It is payable before processing starts and is credited once to the selected preparation route for the same accepted scope.

AssureRail reconciles declared information to the validated admitted population. Every difference in primary quantity, linked-party quantity, corpus or selected scope is recalculated. There is no tolerance band.

  • Upward adjustment: the additional amount is invoiced and must be paid before the affected report is released.
  • Downward adjustment: the excess first becomes an institutional customer credit. It is not automatically converted into a cash refund.

Reconciliation records the declared, submitted, excluded, duplicate, admitted and billable quantities and the reason for every movement.

4. Customer credit and refund

The workspace remains open if the customer pauses or becomes inactive. At 90 days, dated evidence, cut-off information, provider quotes and buyer requirements must be refreshed before further reliance. Inactivity does not itself create a refund.

Initial Assessment payment is non-refundable for completed processing, customer cancellation, inactivity or a decision not to proceed. A cash refund is considered only for a genuine excess customer credit created by a downward quantity or corpus reconciliation and only when the engagement is formally closed.

Any such cash refund cannot exceed 20% of the original Initial Assessment estimate. The balance remains an institution-level credit for 12 months from formal closure and may be applied to a later eligible AssureRail engagement under the accepted terms. Credit is not transferable to another institution, does not earn interest and cannot offset statutory, tax or third-party amounts unless expressly agreed.

5. Preparation and execution

Portfolio Preparation begins only after the remaining fixed-stage balance for the selected route is paid and required evidence is complete. Professional or additional work outside the accepted SOW requires a change order.

Execution is seller-specific. The fee is calculated on that seller's share of actual purchase consideration successfully settled and is due only on a successful close, subject to the accepted execution mandate and seller-specific minimum. Partial closings under the same mandate use the cumulative ledger stated in the mandate; the calculation does not reset merely because settlement occurs in rounds.

If a seller begins on the standalone route and later appoints AssureRail for the same execution scope, the eligible standalone premium may be credited once against execution fees as stated in the mandate. The credit is capped at earned execution fees, creates no cash refund and does not offset tax, supplier, statutory or additional-service amounts.

A committed-route top-up applies only where the accepted mandate expressly provides for it and the seller voluntarily switches execution elsewhere or withdraws the mandate. Buyer rejection, provider failure or an unsuccessful closing does not alone trigger that top-up.

6. Additional services and external amounts

Base, Premium and Full are comparison views. Only itemised lines accepted in the order, SOW or mandate are contracted.

  • A routine managed service may use a fixed published AssureRail customer rate. AssureRail retains delivery efficiency and absorbs ordinary in-scope overruns.
  • Professional, statutory, duty and exceptional actual-cost items remain separately identified as stated in the quote.
  • A conditional line has zero payable quantity until its stated trigger and approval occur.
  • An unused supplier or statutory reserve is released or credited according to the order; it does not become an AssureRail fee.
  • GST and withholding are applied according to the final invoice and law.

7. Settlement deduction

An earned execution fee or approved closing expense may be deducted from seller proceeds only if the seller-authorised Settlement Distribution Schedule states the exact amount, beneficiary and tax treatment and the appointed bank/provider accepts the instruction. AssureRail does not receive gross consideration, hold customer funds or unilaterally alter the distribution.

8. Order of precedence

The executed MSA, accepted order/SOW, execution mandate and locked Settlement Distribution Schedule control in that order for their respective subjects. This schedule explains the intended commercial mechanics and is not a tax, legal, accounting or buyer commitment.
