Commercial interpretation of the 750-record validation slice

The validation ladder controls how much representative data the model is allowed to claim against; it does not create a separate discount. For a seller below the fixed-stage minima and without a large-programme supplement:

  • committed route total for Initial Assessment plus Portfolio Preparation: ₹8.00L;
  • standalone route total: ₹10.40L;
  • Initial Assessment paid before processing: ₹3.12L, being 30% of the standalone quote;
  • committed Portfolio Preparation balance: ₹4.88L;
  • standalone Portfolio Preparation balance: ₹7.28L.

All figures exclude applicable tax, selected additional services, third-party costs and execution success fees. The admitted count and corpus are reconciled without a tolerance band.

For either of the first two independently approved design-partner NBFC entities, the standard quote remains visible and a lifetime 30% invoice credit applies to the eligible core fees. At the same minimum slice this gives ₹5.60L committed, ₹7.28L standalone and a ₹2.184L Initial Assessment invoice before GST. The discount is entity-bound and does not apply to the current pilot exclusion set of ancillary services, pass-throughs, SFTP, API work, monitoring or contractor day rates.
