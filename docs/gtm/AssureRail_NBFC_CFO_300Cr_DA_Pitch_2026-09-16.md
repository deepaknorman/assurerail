# AssureRail Full — ₹300 crore DA treasury case

**Audience:** NBFC CFO, Treasurer, ALCO, Head of Finance and Head of Treasury
**Status:** Illustrative sales case. Replace the yellow-input assumptions in the supporting workbook with customer data before use in a proposal or board paper.

## The treasury proposition

You have a ₹1,200 crore loan book. You want to release cash from a seasoned ₹300 crore pool without disrupting the remaining ₹900 crore franchise book.

AssureRail Full prepares every admitted loan for buyer review, coordinates the transaction and settlement stack, and establishes the post-close monitoring baseline. On the assumptions below, a par sale produces approximately **₹298.60 crore of net sale cash before GST**, or **99.53% of the ₹300 crore current principal**. If 65% of the pool is supporting existing debt that must be released at closing, the transaction leaves approximately **₹103.60 crore of residual cash**.

That is a liquidity and capital-velocity decision, not free income. The NBFC gives up the future spread on the sold loans. The decision is attractive when the value of immediate liquidity, balance-sheet release, concentration reduction and redeployment exceeds the risk-adjusted value of retaining those future cash flows.

Credit losses, tax, prepayments, late fees and other borrower charges are excluded until actual data is supplied.

## 4. What economic value is surrendered?

If the pool performs to its contractual schedule, the remaining 25 months contain approximately **₹84.15 crore of gross contractual interest**. At 10% funding cost and 1% servicing cost, the modelled remaining contribution is approximately **₹45.58 crore before future credit losses and tax**.

The CFO should therefore compare:

- the risk-adjusted present value of retaining approximately ₹45.58 crore of modelled future contribution;
- the value of receiving approximately ₹298.60 crore now;
- debt and capital released at closing;
- liquidity and ALM improvement;
- concentration and credit risk transferred;
- the return available from redeploying the released cash into new originations; and
- the buyer's actual premium or discount to principal.

The sale case strengthens where the NBFC can redeploy capital rapidly, has a funding or concentration constraint, expects loss deterioration, or obtains attractive pricing for verified PSL assets. The hold case strengthens where the pool is exceptionally clean, funding is cheap and new-origination demand is weak.

## Why the pool may be attractive to a bank

An indicative 80% PSL share would represent up to ₹240 crore of candidate PSL assets, but eligibility must be established loan by loan. Under the [RBI Priority Sector Lending Directions, 2025](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12799), eligible priority-sector pools acquired through direct assignment may qualify for the purchasing bank's relevant PSL category, subject to the applicable conditions and transfer rules. The bank reports eligible borrower amounts rather than any premium paid to the seller. EV status alone does not establish PSL eligibility.

The buyer receives a seasoned pool with observed repayment history and 70% of tenor still available. AssureRail prepares the evidence needed to test eligibility, performance, documentation, concentration, security, servicing and settlement rather than asking the buyer to discover basic defects late in diligence.
