# AssureRail historic-replay partner request pack

**Status:** send-ready draft; no outreach, NDA or customer authority is claimed

**Date:** 4 September 2026

## 1. The narrow request

Ask for permission to reconstruct one already completed conventional transaction under an agreed
NDA and minimised data scope. AssureRail observes the historical evidence and sequence; it does not
move money, title, notices, allotment, register entries or tokens and does not replace the original
decision or record.

The participant receives:

- an evidence/source/authority map;
- an expected-versus-observed timeline;
- an explicit gap and reconciliation-break register;
- a versioned replay dossier and downloadable comparison workbook; and
- a bounded recommendation on whether shadowing a future transaction is worthwhile.

The output is diagnostic, not a legal opinion, retrospective certification, assurance conclusion or
public case study. Nothing is named publicly without a separate written permission.

## 2. DA data-owner message

**Subject:** Private replay of one completed direct-assignment transaction

We would like to reconstruct one conventional DA transaction your institution has already completed,
under NDA and in observe-only mode. Your existing process and records remain authoritative; no live
instruction or transaction action is sent.

We would first hold a 45-minute mapping session with the accountable transaction-file owner. If the
case is suitable, we agree the least data needed, redaction, secure transfer, reviewers, retention
and destruction/return terms before receiving anything. We then return an evidence-graded dossier
and comparison workbook showing what was reproducible, missing, inconsistent or dependent on an
unstructured hand-off.

For the first call, please nominate the treasury/securitisation/operations owner for one completed
transaction and confirm whether the transferor, transferee and source-record owner can review the
result. Please do not email transaction files or borrower information.

## 3. PTC trustee/originator message

**Subject:** Trustee-reviewed replay of one completed conventional PTC transaction

We would like to reconstruct one completed conventional PTC transaction under NDA and in
observe-only mode. The trustee retains its transaction-control authority, and the applicable
RTA/depository/register remains the operative holding/ownership record. AssureRail records and
compares the evidence relied upon; it does not issue, allot, settle or trade anything.

For discovery, we request one trustee operations lead and independent reviewer, an introduction to
the originator/data owner, and identification of the relevant RTA/depository/register route. No
evidence is transferred until the owners, purpose, minimised scope, security channel, retention and
review responsibilities are agreed.

The output is a trustee/participant-reviewed evidence dossier, all-leg timeline and comparison
workbook. A trustee decision cannot conceal a recordkeeper discrepancy; both remain visible.

## 4. First-call qualification

Ask only:

1. Is there one completed conventional DA/PTC case representative of repeat activity?
2. Who owns and may authorise each transaction-file family?
3. Which system or external party held the operative before/after record?
4. Which human reviewers can confirm the historical outcome independently?
5. What material must be pseudonymised, retained in the owner's clean room or excluded?
6. What NDA/DPA, secure transfer, retention, return/destruction and export terms apply?
7. What would make the returned dossier useful enough to shadow a later transaction?

Do not discuss public endorsement, production activation, transaction pricing or replacement of an
incumbent system as a condition of the replay.

## 5. Internal handoff

- PTC uses `docs/templates/AssureRail_Historic_PTC_Replay_Evidence_Intake_v1.md`.

- Relationship owner records the named data owner and permission evidence.
- Product/domain owner confirms the route is compatible before replay authorisation.
- Any unavailable object becomes an explicit gap; it is never replaced by synthetic evidence.
