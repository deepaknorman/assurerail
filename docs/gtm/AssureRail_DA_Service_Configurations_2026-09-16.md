# AssureRail DA service configurations — commercial proposal

**Date:** 16 September 2026
**Status:** Recommended proposal for founder review. This document does not change the accepted rate card, customer contract or production billing configuration.

## Recommendation

Offer three named DA configurations: **Base**, **Premium** and **Full**. Base must be a complete route for a standard, clean direct assignment. Premium adds asset, registry and field work only where the admitted pool actually requires it. Full adds buyer-side coordination that is contractually allocated to the seller and bounded post-close monitoring.

Keep AssureRail fees, external supplier estimates and statutory amounts on separate lines. A configuration defines responsibility and service coverage; it does not convert an unused conditional item into a charge. For each external item, show `included in scope / conditionally required / not applicable`, the source of the requirement, provider, estimate or accepted quote, payer and unused-reserve release treatment.

Reduce the core marginal execution schedule by 10 basis points for competitiveness:

| Settled-consideration slice | Current schedule | Recommended schedule |
|---|---:|---:|
| First ₹10 crore | 50 bps | **40 bps** |
| Above ₹10 crore through ₹50 crore | 40 bps | **30 bps** |
| Above ₹50 crore through ₹100 crore | 35 bps | **25 bps** |
| Above ₹100 crore | 30 bps | **20 bps** |

Retain the proposed ₹5 lakh minimum, cumulative marginal calculation, actual purchase consideration settled as the fee base, and no success fee without a successful close. Fixed-stage pricing remains ₹500 per admitted loan with an ₹8 lakh minimum for the execution-committed route and ₹650 per loan with a ₹10.4 lakh minimum for standalone preparation.

## Risk and coverage comparison

The sales comparison should use **100% population testing** precisely. It means every admitted loan row is processed through the declared computational rules. It does not mean every fact is independently true, every physical asset has been visited or every underlying document has received a separate legal opinion.

| Risk-control question | AssureRail approach | Sampling-led alternative to confirm in competing quote |
|---|---|---|
| Loan population | 100% of admitted unique loan accounts receive a rule outcome | Sample conclusions may be extrapolated to the remaining population |
| Tape reconciliation | Full row counts, duplicates, exclusions, balances and control totals reconciled | May rely on seller control totals plus sample testing |
| Eligibility and exception rules | Deterministic rules applied to the full admitted population, with rule/version retained | Spreadsheet or manual sample; full-population logic may be separately charged |
| Document coverage | Every submitted document and expected-document gap indexed; coverage reported by loan and document type | Sample file review may not expose population-wide missing-document patterns |
| Factual/legal validity | Qualified reviewers examine evidence, material exceptions, assumptions and conclusions; risk-based reperformance remains possible | Professional sample is usually the primary review layer |
| Bureau | Full admitted population only when fresh pulls are authorised and required; otherwise coverage/gaps disclosed | Often sampled or limited to reports supplied by the seller |
| Physical asset/field checks | Risk-based sample or targeted population selected from full-population analytics; never marketed as 100% unless commissioned and evidenced | Sample commonly selected without a preceding full-population exception map |
| Remediation | Versioned gap register, owner, evidence, re-upload and reassessment | Email and spreadsheet follow-up may not preserve a comparable rerun history |
| Settlement | Controlled conditions, authorised instructions, fee/proceeds bridge and reconciliation evidence | Separate adviser, bank and spreadsheet records may need manual reconciliation |
| Post-close monitoring | Full-population feed checks where complete source data is supplied; incomplete feeds remain visibly incomplete | Periodic sample/report review, depending on servicer and trustee scope |

Do not claim that all competitors sample or that sampling is intrinsically inadequate. Ask each provider to complete the same scope matrix and identify its full-population tests, professional sample design, exclusions, reliance terms and evidence retained.

## ₹55 crore, five-seller illustration

Assumptions: five sellers, 825 admitted loans each, ₹11 crore settled consideration each, ₹40 lakh combined fixed-stage fees, and the external-cost working assumptions documented in the EV cohort case. Amounts are before GST.

### Effect of the 10-bps reduction

| Settled consideration | Current execution fee | Recommended execution fee | Reduction |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ₹10cr | ₹5.00L minimum | ₹5.00L minimum | ₹0 |
| ₹25cr | ₹11.00L | ₹8.50L | ₹2.50L |
| ₹50cr | ₹21.00L | ₹16.00L | ₹5.00L |
| ₹55cr | ₹22.75L | **₹17.25L** | **₹5.50L** |
| ₹100cr | ₹38.50L | ₹28.50L | ₹10.00L |
| ₹300cr | ₹98.50L | ₹68.50L | ₹30.00L |

At ₹55 crore, AssureRail fixed-stage and execution fees fall from ₹62.75 lakh to **₹57.25 lakh**, or about **1.04%** of consideration. The successful-execution fee becomes ₹3.45 lakh per seller at equal participation; the five sellers' fixed-stage fee remains ₹8 lakh each.

### Configuration economics

The Base external estimate uses ₹1.65 lakh for fresh bureau pulls, ₹3 lakh for escrow/bank/VAN, ₹10.50 lakh for common transaction counsel and seller schedules, ₹0.53 lakh for ROC work and ₹1.03 lakh for NeSL. The qualified CA-led preparation review and AssureLens baseline are treated as included AssureRail work. A separately relied-upon independent CA report remains a supplier quote.

| Configuration | AssureRail fees | Modelled external/other charges | Working total | Share of ₹55cr |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Base** | ₹57.25L | ₹16.71L | **₹73.96L** | **1.34%** |
| **Premium, before RTO and field quote** | ₹57.25L | ₹24.19L, adding ₹2.48L registry and ₹5L duty | **₹81.44L** | **1.48%** |
| **Premium with one ₹100 RTO action per vehicle** | ₹57.25L | ₹28.32L, before field quote | **₹85.57L** | **1.56%** |
| **Full, first year** | ₹57.25L | Premium items plus buyer counsel and monitoring | **Quote after reporting-unit design** | — |

For Full, use the existing proposed monitoring formula only with an accepted shared-day operating plan: ₹75,000 activation per seller/pool reporting unit, then ₹25,000 per reporting unit per month plus ₹5 per active loan per month. One consolidated 4,125-loan reporting unit costs about **₹6.23 lakh** for activation plus 12 months; five seller sub-pools cost about **₹21.23 lakh**. Adding the ₹4 lakh buyer-counsel base produces a first-year Full increment of approximately **₹10.23–25.23 lakh** above Premium, before GST, RTO and field work.

The Base working total is ₹8.54 lakh below a genuinely all-inclusive 1.5% comparator of ₹82.50 lakh. Premium before RTO and field work is ₹1.06 lakh below it. This advantage disappears if a competing 1.5% quote excludes counsel, escrow, bureau, registry, duty, filings or monitoring; compare identical inclusions and tax treatment.

## Quoting and presentation rules

1. Present the three configurations side by side, followed by a line-item proceeds bridge for the selected configuration.
2. Tag each line as `AssureRail fee`, `estimated third-party pass-through`, `statutory`, or `conditional reserve`.
3. Show Base as the default comparison, never as a preselected order. The customer actively chooses a configuration and provider options.
4. Show Premium and Full conditional items with zero quantity until facts or accepted buyer requirements activate them.
5. Never charge the same review twice. Portfolio Preparation includes qualified expert review; a separate independent reliance report needs a distinct buyer requirement, provider and deliverable.
6. Split AssureLens into a pre-close baseline and recurring post-close service. Do not charge ongoing monitoring in Base.
7. Show full-population computational coverage and professional/physical sampling in separate rows.
8. Release unused statutory and supplier reserves at closing; AssureRail revenue is not increased by an unused external allowance.
9. State that buyer diligence and purchase approval remain with the buyer and no configuration guarantees price, timing or closing.

## Implementation impact after approval

Approval of this proposal requires one versioned commercial-policy change across the pricing engine, execution-fee tests, assessment workspace copy, public EV cohort article, customer quote schema, commercial schedules, revenue workbook and sales/investor decks. Existing accepted quotes and contracts retain their frozen rate-card version. The package selector must store item-level applicability, provider, payer, quote evidence and price rather than only a plan name.
