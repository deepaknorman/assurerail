# AssureRail completed-deal replay proposal — template

**Status:** draft for prospect-specific legal, commercial, privacy and security review

## 1. Parties and purpose

Name the contracting parties, candidate completed transaction, route, purpose, sponsor, data owner
and each participating institution. This engagement reconstructs evidence and workflow only; it does
not amend, execute, certify or give a legal opinion on the completed transaction.

## 2. In-scope proof

- agreed evidence and authority inventory;
- deterministic reconstruction of expected and observed states;
- source, as-of, qualification and integrity recording;
- exception and reconciliation-break register;
- comparison workbook and exportable dossier; and
- read-out against the pre-agreed scorecard.

## 3. Explicit exclusions

- live money, title, issuance, allotment, notice, register or token action;
- credit or investment decision;
- assurance, audit, certification or legal/regulatory opinion;
- production integration or production-readiness conclusion;
- data or functions outside the signed scope; and
- progression to shadow or pilot without a separate written decision.

## 4. Authority, data and security

Record participant permission, trustee authority for PTC, NDA/DPA, purpose limitation, field-level
minimisation, transfer channel, access list, hosting location, encryption, retention/deletion/return,
incident contacts and evidence-export expectations. Public forms and ordinary email are not approved
transaction-data channels.

## 6. Commercial terms

Specify fixed replay fee or funded-design-partner arrangement, payer, currency, taxes, milestone,
invoice timing, cancellation, expenses and ownership of pre-existing/new IP. Do not insert a
transaction percentage unless separately approved for this stage and route.

## 7. Acceptance and next decision

Completion means delivery of the agreed diagnostic outputs with all known gaps retained. It does not
mean the transaction passed, that AssureRail is production-ready or that either party must proceed.
Record one outcome: `ACCEPT_FOR_SHADOW_REVIEW`, `HOLD_WITH_CONDITIONS` or `STOP`.
