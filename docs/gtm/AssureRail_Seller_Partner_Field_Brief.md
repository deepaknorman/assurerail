# AssureRail seller-partner field brief

**Version:** 1.0 — 17 September 2026
**Audience:** approved seller-introduction partner and separately appointed Data Preparer
**Status:** controlled meeting aid; use the current quote and counsel-approved documents for commitments

## The meeting outcome

Leave the room with an honest seller-by-seller tape screen, an analytical cohort range and authority for the next permitted step. Do not leave with a claimed ₹48Cr sale. The language is:

> “₹48Cr is the declared cohort. The tape indicates ₹___–₹___Cr may be eligible today, subject to Tier B evidence and buyer diligence. After counsel-confirmed retention, pricing, debt release, fees and costs, indicative net cash is ₹___–₹___Cr. Each seller closes separately.”

The free Tier A pre-screen accepts only a minimised loan tape. It does not take evidence files, payment, a portal signup, a mandate or an Initial Assessment order.

## Run the meeting in this order

1. **Disclose your roles.** “I may receive a referral fee from AssureRail if eligible AssureRail fees are collected. If you separately appoint me as Data Preparer, you pay that work. I cannot attest your data, accept a quote, pay, submit, approve an outcome or make a buyer decision. Your authorised signatory remains responsible for completeness.” Record acknowledgement.
2. **Define each seller.** Confirm legal entity, declared principal, cut-off date, loan type and seller-specific book reference. Never merge ownership records.
3. **Count the right units.** Count unique seller–loan–borrower and seller–loan–co-borrower triples as primary units; count unique loan–linked-party pairs separately. Capture loan count too, but never price from loans alone.
4. **Run Tier A.** Apply only populated, sourced rules for MHP/MHD, residual tenor, asset/loan type, DPD, restructuring and concentrations. Mark every current exclusion time-cured, remediable or structural.
5. **Show the waterfall.** Put declared → eligible low/high → transferable low/high → indicative net cash on screen. Show cross-seller borrower overlap as an investigation signal.
6. **Explain cost and allocation.** Calculate the cohort once, allocate each seller's amount at formation and show each seller its own frozen amount. A later member withdrawal does not increase a signed seller's allocation.
7. **Agree the next step.** After incorporation/readiness and the applicable documents, each seller separately accepts its quote, pays its Initial Assessment and retains its own mandate and settlement. Do not collect money or imply a binding AssureRail engagement before that gate.

## Make attrition the reason to proceed

Use this script:

> “A smaller eligible pool is not a failed screen. It prevents us from presenting ₹48Cr and withdrawing a third during buyer diligence. Time-cured loans can return when the confirmed holding period is met. Remediable loans can return after data or Tier B evidence is fixed. Structural exclusions stay outside this cohort. We take the credible range to buyers and preserve a dated route back for the other loans.”

Do not say that remediable or time-cured loans will sell later. They must be retested against the then-current rules and buyer requirements.

## Explain the arithmetic correctly

The committed standard calculation is **₹500 × primary units + ₹250 × linked-party units**, subject to **one ₹8L fixed-stage floor for the formed cohort**. The execution calculation remains seller-specific on each seller's actual settled consideration: marginally 40 bps through ₹25Cr and 30 bps above, with one ₹5L cohort floor allocated at formation if the aggregate calculation falls below it. GST, selected additional services and third-party/statutory amounts are separate.

If the fixed calculation is below ₹8L, allocate that cohort floor pro rata by declared primary units. If it exceeds ₹8L, each seller's unit contribution drives its share. Allocate an execution-floor backstop, if relevant, by expected settled share. Freeze the amounts in the separate seller orders at cohort formation; never reallocate a signed seller because another member later changes or leaves. AssureRail bears formation under-fill under the founder decision.

**Seller-allocation example:** with four equal sellers in the ₹5L-ticket illustration, each contributes about 240 loans, 336 primary units and 60 linked units. The ₹8L cohort fixed floor allocates to ₹2L each; an expected ₹7.5Cr settlement creates about ₹3L execution fee for that seller, or about ₹5L core fees before GST and extras. Seller B pays differently from seller A when its counted-unit contribution or expected/actual settled share differs. An approved design-partner coupon would reduce only that approved entity's eligible allocation; it does not change another seller's amount.

### ₹48Cr cohort illustration — standard card, before GST and extras

Assumptions only: 3–4 sellers, even book mix, co-borrowers on 40% of loans, linked parties on 25%, and ₹30Cr total settled. Because each seller's expected settled share is below ₹25Cr, the illustrated execution fee is about **₹12L** in aggregate (40 bps × ₹30Cr); the execution floor does not bind.

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ₹1.5L | 3,200 | 4,480 | 800 | ₹24.40L | ₹12.00L | **₹36.40L** | **1.21%** |
| ₹3L | 1,600 | 2,240 | 400 | ₹12.20L | ₹12.00L | **₹24.20L** | **0.81%** |

This corrects the tempting but wrong shortcut “₹48Cr divided by ₹500.” Loans are not billing units, co-borrowers add primary units, and linked parties have their own units. It also replaces the earlier ₹13.4L whole-cohort illustration: at most two seller entities can receive the design-partner coupon, and only after approval. Never promise the coupon or apply it to the whole 3–4-seller cohort. For an approved design partner, the 30% entity-bound invoice credit is applied to that seller's eligible allocated core fees after allocation; it excludes GST and excluded services/pass-throughs under the current policy.

## Explain three or four closings

The cohort pools preparation and buyer presentation. It does **not** create one legally commingled seller pool. Expect three or four seller-specific ownership chains, representations, mandates, assignments, settlement instructions, invoices and closing records. Propose sequencing the cleanest seller first to prove the data format and closing controls, then reuse the accepted format for the remaining sellers. The buyer and counsel decide whether closings are sequential, conditional or concurrent; never promise a single closing or cross-seller dependency.

## Never promise or imply

- a buyer, named bank, buyer appetite or buyer approval without written authority;
- eligibility, PSL treatment, permitted-transferee status, retention, MHP/MHD, price, proceeds, timing or closing;
- that the ₹48Cr declared amount equals transferable principal or cash;
- that Tier A reviewed RCs, insurance, KYC, stamping, agreements or other evidence;
- that Initial Assessment is human- or expert-reviewed; it is automated and unsigned;
- that Portfolio Preparation replaces buyer, legal, credit, compliance or valuation diligence;
- one legally pooled asset or one closing across multiple sellers;
- a design-partner slot, discount or price until AssureRail records the approved entity-specific position;
- that a cross-seller borrower match proves double financing, fraud or double pledge;
- any regulatory number or interpretation that is not sourced and marked **CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL**;
- PTC, tokenisation, CBDC settlement, a live API connection or ongoing monitoring unless separately contracted and active.

## Escalate before speaking

Stop and obtain AssureRail's approved wording if the seller asks for a named buyer, binding pre-incorporation commitment, regulatory conclusion, exception to the quote, exclusivity, refund, guaranteed slot, evidence review during the free screen or a change in cohort membership after allocation.

**Founder decision still open:** MHP/MHD cures usually take longer than the current three-reassessments/30-day allowance. Do not promise an extension or price. Present the recorded options—longer MHD-only window, low-cost tape-only maturity re-screen, or a separately quoted later run—and escalate for the founder's decision.
