# AssureRail GTM-01 replay and pilot sales kit

**Status:** internal enablement baseline, 3 September 2026
**Claim tier:** design-partner and replay/shadow preparation only
**Customer entry point on publication:** `https://assurerail.com/replay`

## 1. Commercial objective

Win permission to examine one completed conventional DA and one completed conventional PTC—not a
commitment to replace a system or activate a venue. The first sale is a bounded proof that returns a
useful evidence map, exception register and comparison pack even if the customer stops afterward.

The initial proposition is:

> Keep your LMS, trustee, RTA/depository, payment and arranger systems. Let AssureRail reconstruct
> one transaction across them, show which completion facts can be reproduced, and name every gap or
> contradiction before either party considers a live change.

Do not lead with tokenisation, a claim that every incumbent process is email-based, or an accusation
that another platform is conflicted. Start with the prospect's transaction, systems and accountable
owners.

## 2. Ideal first design-partner profile

| Signal | Strong fit | Hold or disqualify |
|---|---|---|
| Repeat flow | Recurring DA/PTC programme with measurable operational burden | One novelty transaction with no repeat path |
| Sponsor | CFO, treasury, structured-finance or transaction-operations owner | Innovation contact without transaction authority |
| Evidence | Closed case with attributable owners and retrievable records | No lawful access or no accountable data owner |
| Counterparties | Willing transferee/investor and, for PTC, trustee/recordkeeper participation | Expects Rail to invent or override another party's evidence |
| Proof discipline | Accepts replay, then shadow, before a live request | Demands production or token claims from a demonstration |
| Control need | Visible evidence, completion, reconciliation or lifecycle break | Only seeks a generic data room or pricing marketplace |

## 3. Discovery meeting structure

### First 15 minutes — transaction and business case

1. Which route repeats: DA, PTC or both?
2. What is the annual number and notional of relevant transactions? Treat answers as confidential
   prospect inputs, not public market evidence.
3. Where does elapsed time, repeated work or uncertainty occur today?
4. Which completed transaction is representative enough to replay?
5. Who owns the transaction file and may authorise its use?

### Next 20 minutes — authority and systems

1. Which institution makes each credit, investment, trustee and operational decision?
2. What record makes completion or holding legally operative for this route?
3. Which systems contain tape, documents, approvals, payment, notice and register facts?
4. Which facts cannot leave a participant boundary and can instead be referenced or attested?
5. Which incumbent platform or provider must remain in the proof?

### Final 15 minutes — proof and next commitment

1. Agree the evidence families and known exclusions.
2. Name business, risk/counsel, operations/trustee and technology/security owners.
3. Agree baseline measures and stop rules before seeing results.
4. Confirm NDA/DPA and secure-transfer route; accept no files in a public form or ordinary email.
5. Schedule a scoping workshop and nominate the case—do not promise a live date.

## 4. Four-part proof programme

| Part | Permitted activity | Customer output | Hard progression gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Replay | Observe-only reconstruction of completed DA and PTC | Source/authority map, timeline, gaps, reconciliation breaks, dossier and workbook | Named data owners and participant/trustee authority |
| 2 · Shadow | Observe a current transaction beside the incumbent process | Divergence report, effort/cycle baseline and target operating model | Accepted replay, security review and independent VAPT/retest evidence |
| 3 · Stress | Exercise timeout, duplicate, ambiguity, restore, exit and reconciliation using approved simulators | Failure/recovery evidence and open-risk register | Provider/customer test authority and safe rollback |
| 4 · Partner-executed pilot | Approved institutions perform every operative act; Rail coordinates and records | Reference transaction and exportable completion pack | Counsel-ratified route/function pack, provider UAT and PR-12 acceptance |

Every part is independently useful and independently stoppable. Progression is a signed decision,
never the automatic result of a successful demo.

## 6. Qualification and CRM stages

| Stage | Entry evidence | Exit action |
|---|---|---|
| `NEW_INQUIRY` | Minimal public form accepted | Named owner reviews within the agreed SLA |
| `QUALIFICATION_REQUIRED` | Institution, route, role and timing present | Confirm repeat flow, sponsor and control problem |
| `REPLAY_DISCOVERY_READY` | Completed case and accountable data owner indicated | Hold discovery; record owners and candidate case |
| `NDA_AND_SCOPE` | Mutual intent and required parties identified | Execute NDA/DPA where applicable; approve data map and transfer channel |
| `REPLAY_SCOPED` | Signed scope, inputs, scorecard and stop rules | Create governed replay case; no live effects |
| `REPLAY_COMPLETE` | Reviewed output and all gaps retained | Customer accepts, holds or stops |
| `SHADOW_SECURITY` | Replay accepted | Vendor security, VAPT and shadow data-minimisation review |
| `SHADOW_SCOPED` | Security and operating owners approve | Observe the nominated current transaction |
| `PILOT_GATE` | Shadow evidence accepted | Counsel/provider/operations/PR-12 decision |

No stage is inferred from email opens, a website visit, software availability or a commercial target.

## 7. Claims and pricing boundaries

- Replay and initial shadow can use a fixed scoped fee or funded design-partner arrangement.
- AssureRail is not a universally fixed-fee service.
- Current internal commercial illustrations are 0.30% for conventional PTC and 0.50% for tokenised
  PTC, subject to route, payer, metric, trigger, caps, refunds, contract and counsel. Do not print the
  rates into a proposal unless the commercial owner approves that prospect-specific use.
- Assurance remains separately appointed and outcome-independent.
- Never claim that software is customer-proven, VAPT-cleared, controlled-live or production merely
  because it is built, deployed or visible.

## 8. Weekly operating metrics

Track counts and elapsed time without converting activity into false evidence:

- qualified institutions by persona and route;
- completed-deal data owners named;
- discovery workshops completed;
- NDA/data-scope packs issued and executed;
- replay cases accepted, held or rejected;
- evidence families received versus expected;
- replay-to-shadow security reviews opened;
- objections and disqualification reasons; and
- time in stage, next owner and dated next action.

Do not treat pipeline notional, projected transactions or website leads as revenue, customers or
validated product-market fit.
