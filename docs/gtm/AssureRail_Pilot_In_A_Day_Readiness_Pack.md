# AssureRail pilot-in-a-day readiness pack

**Status:** operational mobilisation pack; software readiness is not permission to transact

**Owner:** pilot sponsor

**Version:** 1.0 · 7 September 2026
**Applies to:** private product demonstration, completed-deal replay mobilisation and separately
approved shadow evaluation

## 1. What “in a day” can honestly mean

AssureRail can be made ready on short notice for a private, synthetic demonstration or the first
workshop for a completed-deal replay. A real shadow or controlled-live pilot cannot be activated
merely because the software, presentation or contract template is ready.

| Engagement | Can mobilise in one day? | What may occur | Hard boundary |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Private synthetic demonstration | Yes, after the preflight below | Guided product journey over labelled synthetic fixtures | No customer data, regulated act, money, title or external-system claim |
| Completed-deal replay discovery | Yes | NDA/authority scoping and evidence-family mapping | Do not receive files until authority, data scope and secure transfer are approved |
| Completed-deal replay execution | Only if the evidence owner and secure environment are ready | Observe-only reconstruction of a closed transaction | Historical outcome and original records remain authoritative |
| Current-transaction shadow | Usually no | Compare Rail observations with an incumbent process | Needs security acceptance, named owners, route scope and operating approval |
| Partner-executed controlled-live pilot | No automatic fast path | Approved parties perform their own assigned acts | Counsel, VAPT, contracts, external providers and signed activation must all close |

## 2. Twenty-four-hour mobilisation board

### T−24 to T−12 hours — nominate and freeze

- name one pilot sponsor, one demonstration operator and one technical operator;
- select exactly one audience and objective: DA replay, PTC replay, monitoring evidence, or a
  high-level end-to-end synthetic journey;
- record the build commit, environment, operating mode and feature-flag snapshot;
- choose the approved script and synthetic fixture; do not improvise a live-capability claim;
- decide whether the meeting is a demonstration, discovery workshop or data-scope workshop;
- issue the calendar invitation and audience-specific one-page pre-read; and
- freeze the presentation path except for a severity-one security or factual correction.

### T−12 to T−4 hours — prove the environment

- run `npm run demo:preflight` from the standalone repository;
- run the private browser check against the exact target URL and capture the result;
- verify the demonstration gate is non-public, HTTPS-only, no-index and separately credentialled;
- verify demo credentials through the approved private channel, never in the invitation or URL;
- confirm synthetic labels are visible on every relevant screen and export;
- rehearse login, navigation, reset and safe-pause from the presentation device and backup device;
- export a static PDF/screenshot fallback and keep it encrypted locally; and
- verify that no borrower, customer, production key, internal security finding or confidential
  investor material appears in the demonstration.

### T−4 to T−1 hours — commercial and legal readiness

- select the relevant pricing scenario in the governed pricing workbook; do not alter a rate in a
  sent PDF without the commercial owner's approval;
- prepare the draft pilot agreement and statement-of-work schedule with the contracting entity,
  route, mode, exclusions, fees and data scope completed;
- mark all legal documents **DRAFT — SUBJECT TO COUNSEL AND CONTRACT** until counsel and the
  contracting entity approve them;
- for a replay discussion, prepare the historic-replay request pack and evidence intake template;
- for any data discussion, identify the data controller, permitted purpose, secure transfer method,
  retention, return/destruction and incident contacts; and
- confirm whether publicity, logo use, recording and screenshots are prohibited (default: yes).

### T−60 minutes — go/no-go

The pilot sponsor records **GO**, **GO WITH LIMITATIONS**, or **NO-GO** against:

1. correct audience and meeting purpose;
2. exact build and successful preflight;
3. synthetic-only or authorised-data status;
4. operator and backup operator availability;
5. approved claims and pricing materials;
6. contracting/data authority for the activity actually planned;
7. external dependencies required for the selected script; and
8. safe-pause and fallback readiness.

Any unknown in items 2, 3, 6 or 8 is a **NO-GO**. The sponsor signs or timestamps the decision in
the engagement record; a chat message saying “looks fine” is not an activation record.

## 3. The 30-minute demonstration spine

| Time | Demonstration beat | Required talk track |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–3 min | Problem and boundary | One governed case across existing institutions; current demonstration is synthetic and non-transactional |
| 3–7 min | Institution and authority | Identity, membership, mandate and transaction role are separate controls |
| 7–12 min | Route and evidence intake | DA and PTC are distinct; provider material is signed, scoped and still reviewed |
| 12–18 min | Conditions, decisions and rooms | Named access, attributable decisions and open exceptions replace email ambiguity |
| 18–23 min | Completion and reconciliation | Expected, received, verified and reconciled are different states |
| 23–27 min | Lifecycle and monitoring | Coverage, stale inputs and review ownership remain visible |
| 27–30 min | Next proof | Nominate a completed transaction; agree authority and minimum evidence before any upload |

Do not claim customer validation, VAPT closure, legal finality, live matching, settlement, custody or
production availability unless the corresponding accepted evidence exists on the date of the
meeting.

## 4. Carry kit

- presentation laptop, charger and mobile hotspot;
- backup device with encrypted static walkthrough;
- approved HDMI/USB-C adapters;
- current one-page proposition, replay request pack and security/integration one-pager;
- pricing workbook and draft agreement, both marked confidential and versioned;
- blank decision/action log; and
- escalation contacts for product, technical, security, legal/commercial and relationship owners.

## 5. After the meeting — within four business hours

- record attendees, authority, objective, objections, requested proof and next owner/date;
- classify every statement as fact, customer hypothesis, request or open gate;
- do not upload notes containing transaction or borrower information into the public CRM path;
- send only the approved follow-up pack;
- log any factual correction needed in public or protected material; and
- if a replay is proposed, begin with the NDA/authority/data-scope checklist—not a file request.
